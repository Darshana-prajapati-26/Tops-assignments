Q15

Use ChatGPT to suggest a one-liner alternative using map() and reduce() for calculating the total amount spent from an array of transactions (each with amount property), and then implement that one-liner in your code.

💡 ChatGPT Suggested One-Liner

ChatGPT suggestion: chain map() to pull out amounts, then reduce() to accumulate the total — all in one expression.

const totalSpent = transactions.map(t => t.amount).reduce((sum, amt) => sum + amt, 0);
